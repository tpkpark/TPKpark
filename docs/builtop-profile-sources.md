## Identity and visit details

BUILTOP is the display name already used by TPK Park; LocalBusiness schema also records the official legal name, Builtop Group Sdn. Bhd. The guide does not substitute details for the separate Builtop Creation / Steelux listing or an unrelated overseas business using a similar name.

The official contact page does not publish office hours. Other public listings vary: Waze shows 9am–6pm weekdays and Saturday morning, while builtopdesign.com search results show 10am–6pm weekdays. The guide therefore asks visitors to confirm hours and meeting arrangements directly, without fixed opening-hour schema or a claim that appointments are mandatory.

The address and 47100 postcode follow the current official contact page. The Google Maps directions link uses the business name and full address. The site's map embed includes a different area centre, so it is not reused as an unverified pin. No coordinates or place ID are invented.

The main website's About and Our Works navigation links were discoverable, but their destination pages timed out during research. This release links the successfully retrieved homepage, services and contact pages.
