# Wong Shung Yen — 2024 National Art Gallery jade exhibition evidence register

Last reviewed: 12 September 2026

## Scope and conclusion

This register supports the public description of Dato’/Datuk Wong Shung Yen (黄松延) as a co-curator of the 2024 National Art Gallery exhibition **JADE: A Journey Across Millennia of Dedication to Enduring Prosperity / 《璀璨千年—不朽与繁荣之美》 / Pameran Batu Jed: Jejak Beribu Tahun Menuju Kemakmuran Yang Abadi**.

The exhibition was presented at Balai Seni Negara / National Art Gallery, Kuala Lumpur, from 23 November to 23 December 2024, in conjunction with the 50th anniversary of Malaysia–China diplomatic relations. The strongest primary source is the National Art Gallery/MOTAC media release, which explicitly names Wong as **Curator and Special Advisor for the Malaysian Chinese Cultural Society**. Established English, Chinese and Malay publications separately identify him as a curator or co-curator.

### Editorial boundaries

- Describe Wong as **co-curator** of this exhibition, together with Dr Low Cheong Sin (刘创新博士).
- Do not state or imply that Wong owned the 113 works or that the exhibition represented his personal collection.
- Do not state that Wong curated multiple separate jade exhibitions unless a distinct exhibition is independently verified.
- Chinese-media reports give a precise total of **113 works**; official National Art Gallery releases use **more than 100**. Use 113 only when attributed to the Chinese-media record or describe the exhibition more generally as more than 100 works.
- Distinguish primary/independent reporting from rewrites, reposts and syndication when describing the breadth of coverage.

## Recommended public wording

> Dato’ Wong Shung Yen was co-curator, together with Dr Low Cheong Sin, of the 2024 National Art Gallery exhibition *JADE: A Journey Across Millennia of Dedication to Enduring Prosperity*, presented by the Malaysian Chinese Cultural Society in conjunction with the 50th anniversary of Malaysia–China diplomatic relations. His curatorial role is documented by the National Art Gallery/MOTAC and reported across established English, Chinese and Malay media.

This wording is intentionally narrower than claims about collection ownership, personal provenance or multiple exhibitions.
