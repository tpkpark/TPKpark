## Editorial boundaries

The guide retains the directory's Kuche + BaTH styling and explains the KBO / Kuche Bath Outlet alias in visible copy and structured data. It does not change the cluster's existing 16-business count.

The branch landline is +60 3 8079 1268. KBO's website footer links to a general WhatsApp number, 011-3910 1314, also associated with another branch in search results. That number is not described as the Puchong direct contact. Older individual sales contacts are not carried into this guide.

Waze lists daily 9:30am–7:30pm hours, but the current official contact page does not state branch hours. The guide asks visitors to confirm hours and current displays, and omits opening-hour schema. Online categories do not imply every item is in stock or on display. The guide does not promise installation, delivery, warranty coverage or parking availability. It does not reproduce reviews, ratings, promotional prices or retailer superlatives in its editorial copy.

Preparation advice is original visitor guidance: bring layouts and model details, ask an installer to confirm compatibility, and obtain a clear quotation. The `HomeGoodsStore` entry describes an independent business within the park; it does not suggest TPK Park operates the shop.
