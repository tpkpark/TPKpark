## Visit details

The current official contact page supplies the phone. Older search snippets may show +60 12 989 2020; that number is not carried into this guide. The office is not described as a walk-in furniture store or showroom. Visitors are encouraged to arrange a consultation.

Weekday office hours appear in visible text and `OpeningHoursSpecification`. Weekend and public-holiday appointment arrangements appear in all three languages. No fixed opening or closing times are invented for appointment-only days, and they are not represented as permanently closed. Public-holiday appointments take precedence over the usual weekday schedule.

The directions link uses the business name and complete published address. No map coordinates or place ID are invented. The official contact page is linked beside the directions link.

The guide reuses the existing TPK Park image `assets/images/home-living-1120.webp` and its responsive 480px version through the shared image registry.
