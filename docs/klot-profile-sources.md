## Current address, contact and hours

The live official location page gives postcode 47180 and includes Seksyen 2. An older search-engine copy gives 47100; the guide uses the current live page. An older social-media search snippet lists 016-713 5100, which is not the current official contact and is not used.

KLOT's official website publishes Monday–Friday 8:30am–5:30pm and Saturday 8:30am–2pm, with Sunday closed. Visible copy and structured data distinguish the two opening intervals. Visitors are asked to confirm holiday hours and the fittings available to view. The guide does not claim an appointment is compulsory.

KLOT Resources in Puchong is distinct from KLOTZ International, an upholstery business in Johor. No details or photographs from KLOTZ are used.

## Editorial boundaries and integration

The guide covers furniture hardware and storage fittings, rather than presenting KLOT as a general furniture showroom. Its directory category is refined to Cabinet Hardware / Kelengkapan Kabinet / 橱柜五金. This remains one of the existing 16 businesses; the historical Malaysia Book of Records figures of 96,728 sq ft, 16 businesses and eight categories are unchanged.

Measuring internal cabinet space, trying mechanisms and agreeing installation responsibilities are original visitor advice. No claims are made about stock, model load ratings, prices, installation inclusion, warranty duration or a commercial partnership with TPK Park.

All three directories link to the matching language guide. The existing brand layout supplies heading-to-paragraph spacing and aligned advice-card headings.
