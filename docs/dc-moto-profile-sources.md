## Centre, dealers and hours

DCMOTO's contact page explicitly directs sales and purchases to dealers. The page therefore describes an Experience & Service Center, with `LocalBusiness` schema, and sends sales enquiries to the official dealer directory. It does not promise direct retail purchases, same-day installation, free consultations, stock or walk-in demonstrations at 49G.

The Klang Valley dealer list also includes DC Global Auto Distributor in Bukit Jalil and Gets Distribution in Klang. Their phone numbers and addresses must not be substituted for the Puchong centre's contact. Older searches mentioning dealer numbers are not used.

Opening hours are not published in the official contact listing. No opening-hours schema is added; visitors are asked to confirm hours, support and any demonstrations with the centre before travelling.

The centre's published number is labelled WhatsApp. Its visible link opens WhatsApp rather than a telephone dialler. A `ContactPoint` records that channel in structured data. The shared visitor template now supports an optional `phoneUrl`; other guides retain their telephone links. This does not introduce a WhatsApp channel for TPK Park management.
