## Editorial boundaries

The official contact page is the source for the branch landline. Niro Granite lists a different mobile number, 016 210 2678; that number is not published in this guide.

The official catalogue's header gives Monday–Saturday, 9am–7pm for Puchong. Niro Granite's listing gives Saturday–Thursday, 10am–7pm. The guide therefore asks visitors to confirm hours and omits opening-hour schema. It does not repeat promotional prices, stock labels, boilerplate return promises, or claim that the full online catalogue is on display at this branch.

Visiting advice is original editorial guidance: bring measurements and references, compare fittings and finishes, and confirm compatibility with the retailer and installer. Delivery, installation terms, warranties and parking availability are not promised. `HomeGoodsStore` describes the branch within the park; it does not imply TPK Park owns or operates the retailer.
