## Editorial boundaries

The guide adds a page for an existing directory business and retains the cluster's 16-business count. It describes an independent showroom within the park, without suggesting TPK Park operates the store.

Opening hours and current displays should be confirmed with the branch. No current prices, stock quantities, installation services, parking availability, warranties, reviews or ratings are asserted. Retailer superlatives are not repeated. Range descriptions are factual and brief.

The room-plan, suitability and order-checking suggestions are original editorial advice. Visitors are asked to consult their supplier and installer about product suitability, quantities, cutting allowances, batch consistency and installation materials; the guide does not supply technical specifications or a fixed wastage percentage.
