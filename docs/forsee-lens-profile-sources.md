# Forsee Lens guide sources

Checked 16 September 2026. Scope: Forsee Lens / Forsee Vision Care at Taman Perindustrian Kinrara, Puchong.

## Business identity and location

- Forsee’s current official website identifies the business as **Forsee Lens / Forsee Vision Care** and repeatedly gives the Puchong contact address as `No.71, Jalan TPK 2/8, Taman Perindustrian Kinrara, 47180 Selangor`.
- The official footer identifies **Eyepoint Technology Sdn Bhd** at the same No. 71 address. The guide therefore presents Forsee Lens as the public-facing brand and Eyepoint Technology Sdn Bhd as the company behind the published contact base.
- A current employer profile for Eye Point Technology independently describes the company as an optical-lens manufacturing business at No. 71, Jalan TPK 2/8, Taman Perindustrian Kinrara.

## Business type and scope

- Forsee’s official About page describes the Puchong operation as a fully automated in-house ophthalmic lens lab producing freeform-design lenses and advanced lens technology.
- The guide deliberately does **not** describe the TPK Park premises as a conventional retail optician because the official site is centred on lens manufacturing, lens technology and eye-care-professional partnerships rather than a consumer retail-store service menu.
- Public product categories include `Above 40`, `Young Adult`, `Kids`, `Metalfilm`, `Blue Defend` and `Photoshift`. Current product pages also include MYOBOOST PLUS and progressive/freeform lens designs.
- Marketing superlatives from the brand’s own website are not repeated as independent claims.
