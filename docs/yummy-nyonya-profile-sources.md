# Yummy Nyonya Kitchen guide sources

Checked 16 September 2026. Scope: Yummy Nyonya Kitchen at Taman Perindustrian Kinrara, Puchong.

## Business identity and location

- A published merchant-list PDF records `yummy nyonya` / `yummy nyonya kitchen` at `43g, 43, Jalan Tpk 2/8, Taman Perindustrian Kinrara, Puchong, Selangor, Malaysia`. This is the strongest exact-unit evidence for the branch and is used for the TPK Park guide.
- Waze independently lists **Yummy Nyonya Kitchen** on **Jalan TPK 2/8, Puchong**, supporting the road identification.
- BizMalay currently lists the business as `43G, Jalan Tpk 2/7` with phone numbers `011-1163 1126` and `010-891 2102`. The unit number and phones are useful corroboration, but its road suffix conflicts with the merchant-list record and Waze. The TPK Park guide therefore uses **43G, Jalan TPK 2/8** and documents the older `2/7` discrepancy rather than repeating it as current.
- A 2021 Puchong restaurant roundup also lists Yummy Nyonya Kitchen on Jalan TPK 2/7 with the same primary phone. Because it predates the other evidence and gives a broad `31-57` address range, it is treated as historical/secondary location evidence only.

## Operating hours

- BizMalay currently lists Monday–Saturday `06:00–17:30` and Sunday closed.
- The older Puchong roundup lists Monday–Saturday `08:00–16:30` and Sunday closed.
- The two sources agree that the business is primarily a daytime operation and generally closed on Sunday, but their exact Monday–Saturday hours conflict. The page therefore gives cautious daytime guidance and asks visitors to confirm before a time-sensitive trip. Exact opening hours are not encoded in Restaurant structured data.
