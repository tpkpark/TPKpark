# Perodua 3S Kinrara visitor guide

Routes: `/automotive/perodua-3s-kinrara/`, `/ms/automotive/perodua-3s-kinrara/` and `/zh/automotive/perodua-3s-kinrara/`. Checked on 16 September 2026.

## Branch identity and contacts

The [Perodua 3S Kinrara branch website](https://www.perodua3skinrara.com/) names the operator as **Lon G Setia Auto Sdn. Bhd.** and provides separate sales and service departments. The actual public phone links, read from the rendered page, are `tel:0332912266` for sales and `tel:0332162255` for service. The guide uses their international equivalents, **+60 3 3291 2266** and **+60 3 3216 2255**.

The site identifies Taman Perindustrian Kinrara, Seksyen 2, 47180 Puchong. The unit address is **8, Jalan TPK 2/3**, also published in [sales-adviser location material](https://peroduabyakmal.com/) and public posts displaying the branch sign. The branch's own [Google directions](https://maps.google.com/?daddr=3.047798,101.637174) and [Waze directions](https://ul.waze.com/ul?ll=3.04748271%2C101.63726807&navigate=yes&zoom=17&utm_campaign=default&utm_source=waze_website&utm_medium=lm_share_location) are retained.

The branch publishes these distinct schedules:

- Sales showroom: Monday–Saturday, 8:30am–8pm; Sunday and public holidays, 10am–4pm.
- Service centre: Monday–Saturday, 8:30am–5pm; closed on Sunday and public holidays.

Its [online service booking page](https://www.perodua3skinrara.com/onlineservicebooking) repeats the service hours and explains that an adviser calls to confirm appointment requests. The guide preserves that distinction; submitting a form is not described as an automatically confirmed booking.

The manufacturer’s older sales/service PDF lists still show this operator's Kayu Ara location. Those addresses and telephone numbers are not reused. Individual sales-adviser numbers, promotional prices, finance rates, warranties and delivery promises are not carried into this branch guide.

## Editorial and site integration

The guide belongs to Automotive, with a link from that directory in every language. It contains no peer-brand links or nearby-brand recommendations. Historical Home & Living recognition figures are unchanged.

Visit preparation is original editorial guidance: bring the information relevant to a showroom, service or parts enquiry, and confirm scope, availability and cost with the branch. It does not claim particular current stock, a free test drive, guaranteed parts availability or workshop capabilities beyond the published 3S scope.

The `AutoDealer` identifies the operator, address, sales number and showroom schedule. A nested `AutoRepair` department carries the separate service number and schedule. Both contacts and both sets of hours are visible on the page and available to Ask TPK Park from public page content.

Shared visit rendering gains optional labelled contact and hours rows while preserving the existing single-contact output for other guides. Advice headings remain aligned and the shared 20px visit-heading spacing is retained.

The route is included in canonical and language metadata, breadcrumbs, sitemap, public assistant sources and bounded analytics. Sales and service calls have distinct targets. Branch website and service-request links, Google Maps and Waze are tracked without telephone numbers, coordinates, query values or fragments in event payloads.
